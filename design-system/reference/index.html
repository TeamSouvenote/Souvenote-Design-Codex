A system worth keeping

The visual language of Souvenote's landing page: warm ivory, dusty berry, forest green and antique gold. Light Lato with one thin script word. Everything here uses tokens/tokens.css. Guidelines are in docs/.

Colour palette

About 70% ivory, 20% forest and 10% accents. Gold always means "AI made this". Berry carries emphasis. Terracotta is decoration only.

Spacing & shape

A 4px base. Gutters are 20, 32 and 48px, and section spacing 64, 80 and 96px, by breakpoint. Content stays within 1200px. Everything clickable is at least 44px.

Engraving motifs

Line art in espresso at 55%. Use few of them, use them large, and place them only in space that's guaranteed empty. They scroll with the page and are hidden from screen readers.

Shown at 100px. On the page they are 72-150px wide and tilted between -14° and +10°. See docs/03-patterns.md for where each one goes.

The logo

Antique gold on forest only. The app shows pre-tinted gold PNGs as plain images (never a CSS mask), so the logo stays visible with reduced motion and high contrast. The same files are in assets/.
