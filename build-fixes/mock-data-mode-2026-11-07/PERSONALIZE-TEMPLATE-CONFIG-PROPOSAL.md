## The three draft cards

| | Horoscope | On This Day | Comic Card |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Order** | Names → birth month/day (year optional) → photo choice → sign-specific choices | Names → full birth date → optional city or country → story candidates → headline | Names → photo or description → occasion (if unknown) → story branch → storyboard → comic style |
| **Photo modes** | No photo, Transform, Enhance, Decorate | No photo (recommended), Transform, Enhance, Decorate | No photo, Transform |
| **Own steps** | Sign worked out by the app. Three Transform ideas tailored to the sign. Enhance styles that fit the sign. Birthday message branch (a trait, a memory or joke, or a simple wish). Cover line. | Verified story candidates with sources (mocked). Pick one. Three headlines (playful, warm, classic). Masthead, date and optional factual caption. | Story branch (memory or joke, everyday habit, invented adventure). A 4-panel storyboard (at most 2 bubbles per panel, about 8 words each). Three comic styles. |
| **Default orientation** | Portrait 5×7, can switch | Portrait 5×7, can switch | Portrait 5×7, can switch |
| **Song** | Song-prompt slot (empty) | Song-prompt slot (empty) | Song-prompt slot (empty) |

**Song for all three:** Vocal, Instrumental or No song. The song step asks whether to use the recipient's name, and whether to mention the card's detail (the trait or memory, the story, or the comic).

**AI limits:** every suggestion set is an initial draft plus 5 successful rewrites.

**Front text:** the image model draws it all. Review asks the customer to check the spelling.
